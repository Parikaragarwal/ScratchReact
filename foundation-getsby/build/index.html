List of Philosophers

Socrates

Known for his method of questioning and dialogue, which aimed to stimulate critical thinking and illuminate ideas.

Friedrich Nietzsche

Critiqued traditional morality and religion, created the concept of the 'Übermensch' (Overman) and declared 'God is dead.'

Søren Kierkegaard

Considered the father of existentialism, emphasized individual existence, freedom, and choice.
